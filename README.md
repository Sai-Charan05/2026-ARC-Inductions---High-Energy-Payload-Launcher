# 2026-ARC-Inductions---High-Energy-Payload-Launcher

Design a mechanical launcher capable of accelerating a 10 kg payload, and verify that the structure withstands the resulting loads without failure. This task assesses design judgement, load-path reasoning, and your ability to interpret simulation results.

The launching mechanism is left to your discretion. Designs will be evaluated on structural soundness, mechanical efficiency, and originality of approach.

### Design Specifications

- **Payload:** 10 kg concentrated mass (model as a block or cylinder)
- Rigid base chassis
- Moving structural members with a mechanical stop or locking mechanism
- Reinforced pivot/hinge housing
- Energy storage and release mechanism
- Correctly defined rotational and translational joints

### Simulation Requirements

- Constrain the base appropriately. Alternative boundary conditions are acceptable if justified.
- Apply an equivalent dynamic load representing the peak launch forces produced by the 10 kg payload. Where the mechanism includes mechanical stops, account for contact and impact loading.
- Perform a structural stress analysis.

Report on the following:

- Stresses at pivot pins and hinges
- Bending in the primary structural member
- Regions of stress concentration
- Overall factor of safety

> **Note:** If the structure fails before the payload is released, the energy is being absorbed by the frame rather than transferred to the payload. Revisit your load path and member sizing.

### Deliverables

- Fusion 360 source file (`.f3d`)
- Motion animation or screen recording
- Stress simulation screenshots
- Brief write-up (3–5 lines) explaining your design rationale and any notable features
